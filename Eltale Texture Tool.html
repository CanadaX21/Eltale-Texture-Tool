How this works

  
Everything happens in this page, on your computer. Your ROM is never changed and never leaves it.

  
Look around

  
Models has Brian, monsters, bosses and NPCs with their animations. Maps has every room with its doors, actors and sky. Drag to orbit, right-drag or Shift-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. W, A, S, D or the arrow keys fly, E up, Q down, hold Shift to go faster.

  
Change a texture

  
    
    
  1. Pick a model or room, and open the Textures tab on the right.
    2. 
    
  2. Save one texture, or Export all of them, and paint on the copy. Keep its width and height.
    3. 
    
  3. Click the texture and press Replace, or drop your PNG on it. The model changes at once.
    4. 
    
  4. Press Build ROM. You get a new ROM to play.
    5. 
  

  
Change a colour

  
Some textures are grey, and the game colours them. Click one on its model: the Textures tab shows its colours, and you can pick new ones.

  
Your work

  
The Edits tab lists everything you've changed. Choose a working folder there to keep your work as files on your computer; otherwise it stays in this browser. The Textures tab on the left has all 3,300 textures, including the font and menus.
